* 신의 관점 : 면적

### 2.1.1 도별 용도별 집계(결합 확률과 주변 확률의 연습)
```math
\begin{gather*}
P \left( A , H \right) = P \left( A \cap H \right) \\ 
P \left( H \right) = P \left( A , H \right) + P \left( B , H \right) + P \left( C , H \right) \\ 
P \left( A \right) = P \left( A , H \right) + P \left( A , M \right) + P \left( A , F \right) \\ 
1 = P \left( A , H \right) + P \left( A , M \right) + P \left( A , F \right) \\ 
+ P \left( B , H \right) + P \left( B , M \right) + P \left( B , F \right) \\ 
+ P \left( C , H \right) + P \left( C , M \right) + P \left( C , F \right)
\end{gather*}
```
### 2.1.2 도 내 용도 내에서의 비율(조건부 확률의 연습)
```math
\begin{gather*}
P \left( A , M \right) , P \left( B , M \right) , P \left( C , M \right) \\ 
P \left( A , M \right) < P \left( C , M \right) \\ 
P \left( M \mid A \right) = \frac{ P \left( A , M \right) }{ P \left( A \right) } = \frac{ P \left( A \cap M \right) }{ P \left( A \right) } \\ 
\iff \left( P \left( A , M \right) \right) = P \left( M \mid A \right) P \left( A \right) \\ 
P \left( H \mid A \right) + P \left( M \mid A \right) + P \left( F \mid A \right) = 1 \\ 
P \left( H , A \right) + P \left( M , A \right) + P \left( F , A \right) \ne 1
\end{gather*}
```
